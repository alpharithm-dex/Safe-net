# SafeNet brand system

## Positioning

SafeNet makes digital safety easier to reach by placing an optional route to guidance and accountable support at the connectivity moment students already seek.

## Primary line

**Safety where students connect.**

## Product description

SafeNet is a youth-owned, local-first digital-safety gateway. It demonstrates how a participating campus Wi-Fi welcome page can offer practical self-defence guidance, bystander support and consent-led routes to qualified help without requiring another app.

## Voice

- Clear, calm and specific.
- Agency first: use “choose,” “consent” and “you stay in control.”
- Honest about stage and limitations.
- Never sensationalise abuse or promise anonymity, rescue or guaranteed outcomes.
- Refer to unsigned organisations as prospective, never as partners.

## Visual system

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep teal | `#173F42` | Trust, governance and primary dark surfaces |
| Coral | `#E66450` | Urgency, action and key highlights |
| Warm paper | `#F5F1E8` | Primary background |
| Soft gold | `#F3DEB2` | Caution and supporting emphasis |
| Ink | `#17292B` | Body text and structural contrast |

The shield-and-signal mark represents protection at the connectivity layer. Keep clear space around it equal to one quarter of the mark width. Do not place the mark on visually noisy backgrounds or recolour it with gradients.

## Stage language

Use **early functional prototype**. The public site is an interactive demonstration, not a live support service or institutional deployment.
